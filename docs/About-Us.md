# About Us

PharmCAT is developed and maintained by Mark Woon (lead developer), Ryan Whaley (lead developer), Katrin Sangkuhl (lead
scientist) and Michelle Whirl-Carrillo (MPI) at Stanford University. Binglan Li developed the preprocessor. The original
project principal investigators were Teri Klein (current MPI) and Marylyn Ritchie. PharmCAT is part of [ClinPGx](https://www.clinpgx.org).
